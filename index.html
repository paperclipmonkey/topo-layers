4 · Layer heights

A doline only appears if a level falls between its floor and its spill point. Keep terrain smoothing low or shallow ones get flattened away before they can be found.

5 · Geometry

Kerf widens each part by half the beam width. Leave at 0 if your laser software already applies kerf offset.

6 · OSM features

Vector detail from OpenStreetMap, engraved on the layer it falls in (or all on one sheet).

Fetched
—

Names are engraved on whichever layer is the visible surface there, in a single-stroke font that cuts as centre lines. Set in capitals — far more readable engraved small.

7 · Your own points

Load a GeoJSON file. Points are engraved as numbered markers on the layer they sit on, and a CSV key ships with the export so other sheets can refer to them by number.

Loaded

8 · Assembly aids

Holes are sited under the high ground first, deep inside the smallest layers, so one dowel carries as much of the stack as possible. Each sheet is only drilled where a hole clears its edges by the margin.

9 · Nesting

Packs the layers onto stock boards so the whole piece cuts in one job.
